Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • AegisPro v2.0

1. Information We Collect

When you initialize an operator profile or access AegisPro, we collect minimal operational information required to synchronize your study journey:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, Email address, and specialization role when signing up or authenticating via Google OAuth.
  • Study Telemetry & Progress: Checklists, completed learning modules, streak counters, CTF challenge milestones, and UI preferences (theme, audio).

2. Data Storage & Security

Your session and telemetry are persisted locally via client-side IndexedDB and synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore over TLS 1.3 encryption. Security rules enforce that only your authenticated user ID (`request.auth.uid`) has read or write permissions to your records.

3. Third-Party Services

AegisPro utilizes Google Firebase Authentication and Google Analytics to ensure secure sign-in and understand aggregate platform usage. We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user data with advertisers.

4. Your Data Rights

You maintain full sovereignty over your learning data:

  • Data Portability: Export your complete progress history as a standardized JSON backup at any time via the sidebar.
  • Data Erasure: Reset your local and cloud records at any time using the Reset Progress action in your Operator Console.

5. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding your information, contact our security team at privacy@aegispro.dev.
